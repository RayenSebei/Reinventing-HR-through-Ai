# Opening — Prosecution

**1. The schedule cannot carry the scope.** Nothing exists: "no confirmed Vouch code, API routes or schemas are known to exist yet." Yet the team plans to build, in 17 hours, CV ingestion with forensic checks ("white-on-white, tiny font, hidden layers, metadata, prompt injection, keyword stuffing, contradictions"), quote-level evidence dossiers, a weighted ranking, payload-hashed approvals "with expiry, replay protection, single use," a deny-by-default gateway, a hash-chained ledger, four backend-enforced roles, a React frontend and SENTINEL integration. Team size is open ("about four people" vs "a two-person split") and so is the model. Cost: a half-built pipeline, and a broken demo.

**2. The headline demo depends on the least-proven work.** Seventy seconds go to "run 5 SENTINEL scenarios live... and show real pass/fail." But "NO scenario has been executed against a working Vouch API. Vouch's API did not exist when scenarios were written." SENTINEL's results come from "enterprise/finance/SOC, NOT HR" scenarios, are "team-reported, not independently verified," and its own audit admits "simulated human approval is not a real persistent approval." Integration is booked for hours 13 to 14.5. Cost: the differentiator fails on stage or gets faked.

**3. Key spec requirements are left undefined.** The spec demands reliability "on real volumes," but "'Real volumes' is undefined; no batch-size target." It requires "nothing is sent or recorded without human approval," but it is "unclear whether [this] covers AI drafts, uploaded files and logs." CVs come "in French, Arabic, English or mixed," with "no confirmed... French/Arabic/English quality." Cost: judges probe exactly these gaps and find no answer.

**4. The security is checked only against itself.** "Hidden-content detection only expected to be tested on the team's own planted fixtures," and those fixtures were made by Codex with "only artifact checks." The attacker and the defender share authors and assumptions. Cost: the "assurance" in "independent assurance layer" is circular, and that is the core of the pitch.

**5. The startup case has no foundation.** "Incumbents (Workday, Rippling, BambooHR, HiBob) already ship HR AI agents"; "No buyer, price or market claim has been validated." The supporting statistic comes from a "secondary source" in research that is "partly unverified." Cost: the team pitches a company to HR buyers with no evidence that any of them would buy it.
