# Conduct, security and support

## Reporting harassment
Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated. You can report a concern to HR, to your manager's manager, or anonymously through the ethics line listed in the HR portal. Every report is handled confidentially and nobody is penalised for reporting in good faith.

## Phishing and security
Never share your password, even with IT or HR. If an email asks for your login details, payment or urgent action, or comes from a domain that looks almost like ours, do not click its links: forward it to the security team with the report button. Report a suspected incident immediately; you will not be blamed for reporting.

## Personal data
Your personal data is processed only for employment purposes and kept for as long as the law requires. You can ask HR for a copy of your data or for a correction at any time.

## Leaving the company
The notice period is 1 month during the first two years of service and 2 months after that. Your manager and HR plan the handover with you, and you receive an employment certificate on your last day.
