# Leave and time off

## Annual leave
Every employee earns 2 days of paid annual leave per full month worked, which is 24 days per calendar year. Leave is requested in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance and approved by your manager. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; they must be used before 31 March.

## Sick leave
If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. From the third consecutive day of sick leave, a medical certificate is required within 48 hours. Medical details are never shared with your manager; only HR sees the certificate.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive 14 weeks of paid parental leave and other parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Tell HR at least 8 weeks before the leave starts so cover can be planned. Your role is kept for you during the leave.

## Public holidays
The company follows the official public holidays of the country where you work. The list for the current year is published in the HR portal every December.

## Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of up to 3 months can be requested after one year of service. Requests are reviewed by HR and your manager within 15 working days.
