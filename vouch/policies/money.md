# Expenses, training and equipment

## Expenses
Business expenses are reimbursed when they are approved in advance and submitted with a receipt in the HR portal within 30 days. Train travel in standard class is the default. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Training budget
Each employee has a training budget of 1,000 euros per year for courses, certifications and conferences related to their role. Ask your manager to approve the training, then book it through HR. Unused budget does not carry over.

## Equipment
New employees receive a laptop, a headset and a monitor on their first day. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT the same day. Equipment is returned on your last working day.
