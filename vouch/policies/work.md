# Working time and remote work

## Working hours
The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00; outside core hours you may organise your time with your team. Lunch break is one hour and is not counted as working time.

## Overtime
Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance. Each hour of agreed overtime is compensated either as time off or as paid overtime, as you choose, and is recorded in the HR portal within the same month.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week after their probation period, with their manager's agreement. Remote work requires a quiet place, a stable connection and the company laptop with its VPN switched on. Working remotely from another country needs written approval from HR first.

## Probation period
The probation period is 3 months for all new employees. It can be extended once, by up to 3 months, with a written explanation. At the end of probation your manager holds a review meeting with you.
